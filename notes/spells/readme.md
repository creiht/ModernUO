# Spells

ModernUO includes **~135 spells** across 8 spell schools. Spells are the primary magical abilities available to characters, with varying mana costs, reagent requirements, cast times, and effects.

Spellcasting relies on the Magery skill (and sometimes related magical skills). The chance to successfully cast a spell and its effectiveness are determined by the caster's skill level, the spell's requirements, and various modifiers from equipment (FCR, mana leech, etc.).

## Spell Schools

### [Magery](magery.md) — 64 spells
The foundational magic school, covering all 8 circles of traditional magic. Includes attack spells, healing, summoning, and utility magic.

### [Chivalry](chivalry.md) — 11 spells
Paladin magic focused on holy power, protection, and divine intervention.

### [Bushido](bushido.md) — 8 spells
Samurai martial arts techniques that blend physical and spiritual power.

### [Ninjitsu](ninjitsu.md) — 10 spells
Ninja combat techniques emphasizing stealth, agility, and shadow arts.

### [Necromancy](necromancy.md) — 19 spells
Dark magic of the undead, including summoning, life drain, and curse spells.

### [Mysticism](mysticism.md) — 9 spells
Gargoyle magic tied to elemental forces and the afterlife.

### [Spellweaving](spellweaving.md) — 13 spells + 3 base classes + 2 items + 3 mobile classes
Ancient magic from the Isle of Silk, featuring unique spell mechanics.

### [Gargoyle](gargoyle.md) — 1 spell + traits
Gargoyle racial abilities including the Fly spell.

## Key Concepts

- **Mana**: Each spell costs mana to cast; higher circle spells cost more
- **Reagents**: Materials required for most spells (bones, garlic, nightshade, etc.)
- **Cast Time**: Delay between command and spell execution
- **Recovery Time**: Delay after casting before next action
- **FCR (Faster Cast Recovery)**: Reduces cast time and recovery time
- **Mana Leech (MLC)**: Returns mana on successful hits
- **Spell Channels**: Some spells require channeling (continuous concentration)
