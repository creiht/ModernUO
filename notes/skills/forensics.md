# Forensics

**Forensic Evaluation (Forensics)** is used to investigate crime scenes and determine cause of death. It reveals information about how a creature died.

**Title:** Detective | **Primary Stat:** Intelligence | **Secondary Stat:** Dexterity

**Source:** `Distribution/Data/skills.json`, `SkillsInfo.cs`

## Mechanics

- **Stat scaling:** +0.02 Dex and +0.08 Int per point of those stats
- **Gain rates:** 0.2 Dex gain, 0.8 Int gain per use
- **Primary use:** Investigating death scenes

---

## Expansion Notes

Forensics is available from character creation in the base game. No expansion is required.
