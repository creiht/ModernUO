# Carpentry

**Carpentry** is the skill used to craft wooden items including furniture, barrels, doors, and various containers. It shares the highest Strength gain rate with Mining and Lumberjacking.

**Title:** Carpenter | **Primary Stat:** Strength | **Secondary Stat:** Dexterity

**Source:** `Distribution/Data/skills.json`, `SkillsInfo.cs`

## Mechanics

- **Stat scaling:** +0.2 Str and +0.05 Dex per point of those stats
- **Gain rates:** 2.0 Str gain, 0.5 Dex gain per use (highest Str gain, tied with Mining and Lumberjacking)
- **StatTotal:** 25 (tied highest among crafting skills)
- **Primary use:** Crafting wooden items from lumber resources

Carpentry is one of two crafting skills with a StatTotal of 25, tied with Cartography and Tailoring.

---

## Expansion Notes

Carpentry is available from character creation in the base game. No expansion is required.
