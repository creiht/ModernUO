## Overview

When creating a character in ModernUO, you'll choose from multiple races (Human, Elf, Gargoyle) and professions (Paladin, Samurai, Ninja, Necromancer, Generic). Your stat allocation, skill selection, and starting gear all depend on these choices.

The core stats — Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence — govern everything from damage output to mana capacity. Understanding how these interact with skills, combat, and spellcasting is essential for building an effective character.

Movement in ModernUO spans six distinct maps, each with unique terrain, creatures, and resources. The direction system and running mechanics form the basis of exploration.
